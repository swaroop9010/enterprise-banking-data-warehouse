# Data Dictionary

This document defines every entity used in the Enterprise Banking System.

---

## Customer

Stores customer information.

---

## Account

Stores customer bank accounts.

---

## Transaction

Stores deposits, withdrawals and transfers.

---

## Loan

Stores customer loan information.

---

## Credit Card

Stores customer credit card details.

---

## Branch

Stores bank branch information.

---

## Employee

Stores employee information.

---

## Fraud Alert

Stores suspicious transaction alerts.
