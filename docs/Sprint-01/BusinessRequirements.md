# Enterprise Banking System

## Business Requirement

The banking system should support:

- Customer Management
- Account Management
- Transaction Processing
- Loan Management
- Credit Card Management
- Fraud Monitoring
- Branch Management
- Employee Management

---

## Business Entities

1. Customer
2. Account
3. Transaction
4. Loan
5. Credit Card
6. Branch
7. Employee
8. Fraud Alert

---

## Customer Entity

### Purpose

Store customer information.

### Business Rules

- Customer ID must be unique.
- Every customer must have a first name.
- Every customer must have a last name.
- Every customer must have one phone number.
- Every customer must have one email.
- Customer must be at least 18 years old.
- Customer status can only be Active, Inactive, Closed, or Pending.
